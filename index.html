About

I am currently a PhD student in Quantitative Methods at Purdue University's Daniels School of Business.

Before joining Purdue, I studied Economics at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and spent an exchange semester at McGill University. My research focuses on mathematical models of individual choice, stochastic choice, and optimization.

Research Interests

Choice Modeling

Random utility models, multinomial logit models, mixture choice models, and stochastic choice.

Optimization

Convex optimization, mathematical programming, polyhedral methods, and computational optimization.

Decision Theory

Axiomatic foundations of choice and behavioral representations of decision making.

Research Projects

Choice Branching Models

Working Paper

A stochastic choice framework in which decision makers sequentially search through alternatives and probabilistically branch when preferred alternatives are unavailable.

The model connects search behavior, memory, response time, and classical stochastic choice models.

Geometry of Stochastic Choice Models

Ongoing Research

Studying geometric and polyhedral representations of Random Utility Models and finite-mixture Logit models, with applications to model identification and estimation.
